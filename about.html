About

Built on the ground, in Northern Karnataka.

Kaveri Advisory grew out of an existing geofencing and plot-digitisation effort across the sugarcane belt supplying Godavari Biorefineries Ltd.

Background

Why this exists

Sugarcane cultivation is water-intensive, and irrigation scheduling has long depended on farmers' individual experience rather than measured soil and weather conditions. Across an ecosystem of 18,000–25,000 farmers, that gap shows up as water wastage, avoidable yield loss and inefficient use of scarce irrigation power.

Thousands of plots in the region were already digitised and geofenced, and soil test and farmer records were already collected digitally. Kaveri Advisory was built to put that existing data to work — turning it into a recommendation a farmer can act on, plot by plot.

Sensor observations, satellite data and weather forecasts, brought together to improve irrigation efficiency and support sustainable sugarcane cultivation.

Principles

How we think about AI on the farm

The system is built to support decisions, not replace the people making them.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Transparent recommendations

Every advisory is interpretable — farmers and agronomists can see why a recommendation was made.

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Expert override

Agronomists and field supervisors can review, adjust or reject any AI-generated recommendation.

PRIVACY

Data protection first

Farmer, plot and sensor data is handled under role-based access and encryption, aligned with the DPDP Act.

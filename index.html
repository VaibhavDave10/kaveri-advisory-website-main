The problem

Sugarcane runs on experience.
It should run on evidence.

Irrigation timing is usually a judgement call, not a calculation. Across thousands of spatially scattered plots, that judgement call adds up to real losses.

01

Water wastage

Over-irrigation driven by habit rather than actual soil moisture readings.

02

Yield loss

Moisture stress from delayed irrigation quietly erodes cane tonnage.

03

Wasted power

Pump hours scheduled around habit instead of electricity availability windows.

04

Blind monitoring

No practical way to track soil and crop condition across 18,000+ scattered plots.

The approach

Four data streams. One recommendation.

Field sensors, satellite and weather data, and years of farm records feed a set of prediction models that convert into one thing a farmer can act on: a clear irrigation instruction.

IoT

Field sensors

Soil moisture, temperature and humidity, collected from geofenced plots and streamed over low-power IoT gateways.

GEOSPATIAL

Plot mapping

Digitised plot boundaries and satellite-derived crop health indices, tied to each farmer's registered land.

WEATHER

Forecast data

Rainfall and micro-climate forecasts that adjust irrigation timing automatically when rain is on the way.

FARM RECORDS

Crop history

Soil tests, variety, planting date and past yield, used to personalise every recommendation per plot.

ML MODELS

11 prediction models

From next-irrigation-date to yield-loss forecasting — see the full model line-up on the solution page.

LLM

Plain-language advisory

Technical predictions rewritten as short, farmer-friendly advisories in English, Kannada, Marathi and Hindi.

Who it's for

Decision support for everyone on the farm side of sugarcane.

Farmers get a clear next action. Field teams get a way to monitor thousands of plots at once. Factory management gets a clearer picture of cane availability ahead of crush season.
